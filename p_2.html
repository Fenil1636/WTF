About the Department

➜The Computer Engineering program at DEPSTAR focuses on developing strong technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities, and practical skills required for the modern technology industry. The program combines concepts of computer hardware, software, programming, mathematics, and information systems to prepare students for a wide range of careers in the technology sector.

➜Students gain knowledge in areas such as Programming, Data Structures, Database Management, Computer Networks, Operating Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Web Development, and Software Engineering. The department also emphasizes practical and project-based learning, workshops, seminars, industry interaction, and career development.

➜At DEPSTAR, students are encouraged to participate in technical projects, hackathons, research activities, internships, and skill-development programs. This helps them develop both technical expertise and professional skills needed to solve real-world problems.

Research & Innovation Center

➜The Research Center at DEPSTAR is dedicated to fostering innovation, creativity, and advanced research in the field of Computer Engineering. It provides students and researchers with an environment where they can explore emerging concepts, experiment with new ideas, and develop innovative solutions to real-world challenges. The center focuses on areas such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Robotics, Cybersecurity, Blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), and Computer Vision. Through research projects, technical experimentation, collaborative learning, and practical applications, students are encouraged to transform their ideas into meaningful technological solutions and contribute to the future of computing and digital innovation.

➜🤖 Artificial Intelligence: Exploring intelligent systems that can learn, analyze information, and solve complex problems using modern AI techniques.

➜🦾 Robotics: Researching intelligent machines and automated systems designed to perform tasks efficiently and interact with their environment.

➜🔐 Cybersecurity: Developing technologies and techniques to protect computer systems, networks, applications, and data from cyber threats.

➜⛓️ Blockchain: Exploring decentralized technologies that provide secure, transparent, and reliable ways to store and exchange digital information.

➜📡 Internet of Things (IoT): Connecting smart devices and sensors to collect, exchange, and analyze data for innovative real-world applications.

➜👁️ Computer Vision: Developing systems that enable computers to understand and interpret images, videos, and visual information.
